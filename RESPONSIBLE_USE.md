# Responsible Use

Agent Safety Jailbreak Lab is provided for authorized research and local evaluation of instruction-following behavior.

Acceptable uses include:

- Testing models and accounts you own or are authorized to assess.
- Comparing how assistants interpret user-level instructions and output styles.
- Evaluating prompt robustness in isolated or non-production environments.
- Developing mitigations, documentation, and safer configuration practices.

Do not use the project to:

- Access systems, data, or accounts without authorization.
- Evade authentication, billing, permissions, or organizational controls.
- Exfiltrate secrets or conceal malicious activity.
- Claim that local instructions override provider-enforced safeguards.

Output styles and user-level instructions influence model behavior but do not remove platform policies or guarantees. Test results should be reported accurately and with enough context to reproduce them.
